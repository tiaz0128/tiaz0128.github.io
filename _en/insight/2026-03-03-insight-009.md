It's already March 2026.

Truth is, I jotted down a quick 2025 retrospective on Instagram last year but never wrote down my 2026 goals, so I'm only getting to it now. Maybe it's because, looking back, I've realized that when you write your goals down, they tend to come true...

So I'm writing them down, with a promise to myself to make them happen.

### 2025, a Year of Meeting Many People

> "I don't have any seniors, senior engineers, or mentors to look up to"

There's no perfect mentor. But... 'find something to learn from each and every person you meet.' If you hold on to each of those lessons, I'm convinced that the ideal mentor you once pictured will one day turn out to be you. Go meet and talk with all kinds of people. Beyond tech, you can learn from their thinking, their mindset, and their experience.

## 2026 Plans

In 2026, I plan to study cloud with Kubernetes at the center. And of course, I'll keep up my community activities, which I can't do without.

- Study Kubernetes
- Study a variety of clouds
- Create an Inflearn course
- Community activities

## Studying Kubernetes

The topic I'll dig into most deeply this year is, without a doubt, Kubernetes. When you study cloud, the technology you end up meeting at the end is container technology.
It's unavoidable, so in 2026 I plan to pour almost all my time into studying Kubernetes.

- Study Kubernetes in depth
- Go for the Kubestronaut title (earn 5 certifications)
- Learn Go (great for working with Kubernetes natively)

![kubernetes](/assets/img/content/insight/009/012.webp){:.img-200x200}

## Studying a Variety of Clouds

If last year was about getting a feel for how to study through the easiest certifications, this year I plan to study by going one step up, taking on 'Associate' or 'Professional' level certifications. Starting with AWS, I want to take another shot at certifications from each provider.

- [SAP-C02: Certified Solutions Architect - Professional](https://aws.amazon.com/ko/certification/certified-solutions-architect-professional/){:target="_blank"}
- Certifications from other providers

![AWS SAP](/assets/img/content/insight/009/011.webp){:.img-200x200}
