## About K-DEVCON

[Sungwook Kang](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sungwook-kang/){:target="_blank" .none} opened with a brief introduction to K-DEVCON.

- The **small spark** that started the community
- Started in 2016 in LA, California; next year it'll be a 10-year-old community...!
- Renamed to 'K-DEVCON' in 2021, currently rebranding
- A **sandbox-like community** where you can take on challenges and grow safely

What resonated with me most were the 'small spark' that started it all and the idea of a 'sandbox-like community'.

## How the Community Began

Sungwook said that back when he was inexperienced, an older friend helped him a lot. And one day he suddenly wondered, 'Why is this person doing so much for me?', so he asked:

> "Why do you go this far for me?"<br/>
> "Because it's fun! Help someone else later on, too."

## Why Do They Do It?

As I went to event after event, at some of them my eyes kept drifting to the people running the event. And I got curious.

> "Why do those people go that far?"

Every one of them said it wasn't their day job. When I asked why they pour in time and effort for something that isn't even their job, the answers were all pretty similar.

- Because they like it
- Because it's fun
- Because they wanted to try it
- Because they just want to help

Communities made of people like that seem to have a special warmth. Maybe that's why I felt that same warmth at K-DEVCON 2025.

## Why Communities Matter

What I've noticed from going to many events is that there seem to be roughly two kinds.

- Company-led events
- Community-led events

### Company-Led Events

When I first thought of events/conferences, I pictured a grand building, countless booths, prizes...! 'Company-led events', basically. They have their good sides, but it's a pity that if a company doesn't host one, there's simply nothing you can do.

### Community-Led Events

'Community-led events' can feel lacking in some ways. Still, I think they have more upsides!

- A welcoming, warm atmosphere
- More emphasis on connecting attendees with each other
- A virtuous cycle where members share their experiences and also run things

As long as there are members, even with limited resources it somehow keeps going. Isn't that the biggest charm? Maybe that's why every community feels precious to me, regardless of how well-known it is or how big its events are.

## My Hopes Going Forward

Me included, I'd guess 90% of community members are introverts. Even so, I strongly recommend joining a variety of communities. Communities, too, **need your interest and participation more than anything**{:.orange}.

And I'm sure the community you find will welcome you warmly and **help you grow by acting as a safe sandbox.**
