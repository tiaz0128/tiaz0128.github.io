## NHN Cloud Technical Certifications

NHN Cloud certifications come in three levels.

- **CES(Cloud Essentials Essential)**
- CAA(Cloud Architect Associate)
- CAP(Cloud Architect Professional)

![NHN Cloud certifications](/assets/img/content/insight/005/004.webp){:.img-s}

`> NHN Cloud certifications come in three levels`{:.img-caption}

## Exam Overview: NHN Cloud Essentials (CES)

![NHN Cloud certification](/assets/img/content/insight/005/001.webp)

- Exam: [NHN Cloud Essentials(CES)](https://www.nhncloud.com/kr/edu/certification/essential){:target="_blank"}
- Date and time: 2025-02-17 19:00
- Fee: 50,000 KRW (I got a 10,000 KRW discount coupon!)
- Duration: 90 minutes
- Questions: 60
- Passing score: 700 / 1000
- Results: 10 a.m. the next day

For details, see the [NHN Cloud Essentials Exam Guide](https://rlutbig4t.toastcdn.net/static/exam-guide/nhn-cloud-certified-exam-guide.pdf){:.none target="_blank"} and the [Customer Center FAQ](https://www.nhncloud.com/kr/support/faq){:.none target="_blank"}.

## How the Exam Works

{% include template/alert.html
  type="tip"
  about="Three days before the exam, you'll receive an 'Online Exam Room Pre-Test' email."
%}

I strongly recommend doing the test connection before you sit the exam.

- Location: Online
- Entry window: you can enter the exam room anytime between 19:00 and 19:30
- Exam time: once you enter and finish the setup, you start right away and have 90 minutes.

![How the exam works](/assets/img/content/insight/005/005.webp){:.img-m}

`> Don't skip the exam room pre-test!`{:.img-caption}

## Things to Watch Out For

On the day of the exam, **you enter through the exam URL sent by email**{:.orange} at 9 a.m. Make sure you keep that email handy!

![Things to watch out for](/assets/img/content/insight/005/002.webp){:.img-m}

`> Keep the email you get on exam day safe!`{:.img-caption}

- You must take it in a private, separate room
- Have your ID ready
- Check your webcam and equipment
- No dual monitors
- You must set up your phone so it shows a side view of you (a stand is allowed); no other items allowed
  - Beforehand, make sure your phone's screen won't turn off during the exam

## How to Study

The best way to prepare for any cloud certification is, of course, to **take a hands-on training course**{:.orange}! On top of that, NHN Cloud Deep Dive is a must-watch, and I'd recommend reading through the official user guide once as well!

- [Offline hands-on training: First Steps with NHN Cloud Hands on Lab](https://www.nhncloud.com/kr/edu/attend/detail/e4fea487-05da-4362-bfd2-6fc4151e7b00){:target="_blank"}
- [NHN Cloud on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@NHNCloud){:target="_blank"}
- [NHN Cloud User Guide](https://docs.nhncloud.com/ko/nhncloud/ko/overview/){:target="_blank"}

## Question Types

Since it's the exam for the most fundamental skills, the questions focused on the core services.

- Multiple choice with 4 options
- Multiple-answer questions (choose 2 of 5)
- **Service identification (find the service that fits the given requirements)**

### Topics Covered

- Compute: instances, images, templates, AS
- **Networking**{:.orange}: VPC, SG, DNS, ACL, floating IP, NAT, IGW, LB ...
- Storage: NAS, Block Storage, Object Storage
- Other services: RDS, Backup, EasyCache
- Organizations / projects
- Terms and conditions

## Final Thoughts

If you focus on the core services, it shouldn't be too hard. As the most basic certification, I think it was set at a fittingly **moderate difficulty**{:.orange}!

I took this exam and passed! When the next-level certifications come out, I'll be back with more. Thank you! 😊
