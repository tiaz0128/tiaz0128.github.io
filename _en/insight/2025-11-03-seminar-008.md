## Google Study Jam: Team Track

Google Study Jam is back!

I remember grinding through the solo Study Jam. Honestly, doing it alone was a really long and lonely process. So this time I joined the **team track**. What made it special this time was that [Seoul Women IT's](https://swits.notion.site/IT-s-04cb12f4b9aa4d0c87b225fc79102c06?pvs=149){:target="_blank" .none} co-ran the team track.

### Leader Selection & Member Applications

First, the leaders were selected, and then each leader recruited the members they wanted. I thought it was a great initiative to **strengthen the hands-on skills of women engineers** and bring more diversity to the ecosystem.

Of course, the study itself was open to anyone (men could join as members too...!). Each leader sent a short survey to the people who applied to their group and made the final picks.

If you're a woman engineer, I highly recommend joining Women IT's. There seem to be a lot of great opportunities!

## How the Study Went

On a sweltering summer day, our three-month study kicked off with a still-awkward first meeting at the ['Hong Geon-ik House in Seochon'](https://hanok.seoul.go.kr/front/kor/life/lifeSarang.do?tab=3){:target="_blank" .none}!

We met online every Sunday at 9 p.m. During the week, everyone worked through Qwiklabs on their own, and on weekends we shared our insights with each other.

Our team leader led us with real dedication. Thank you! And the team members kept at it steadily too. I was truly grateful to see one member join in even while on a business trip abroad.

### While I Was at It...

Since I was doing the Study Jam anyway, I got a GCP certification too. I'll share how I prepared for it in a separate post!

### Personal Project

At the end, we each built a personal project using GCP services based on what we'd studied. It was a great chance to put what we learned to real use!

## Our Own Showcase

We wrapped up the study with our own showcase, back at the 'Hong Geon-ik House in Seochon'. Everyone shared the project they'd built.

### Our Team's Projects

I was amazed that everyone built such creative, cool services in such a short time, making great use of AI!

- A travel destination recommendation service
- A household budgeting app
- An accessibility service for people with visual impairments
- A quiz generator app
- A nutrition analysis and eating-habits coaching service

## Wrapping Up

Honestly, at first I just thought, 'Well, it's got to be better than doing it alone.' But joining the team track turned out to be much better than I expected.

- **Less pressure than going solo**: with teammates, you can keep at it steadily
- **More insight**: even studying the same material, everyone brings insight from a different angle
- **The project**: would I really have built one on my own?
- **Final presentations**: I got to join the final presentations, plus swag as a bonus...!

![alt text](/assets/img/content/insight/008/012.webp){:.img-m}

`> Doing it together really is more rewarding than doing it alone`{:.img-caption}

If there's a team track, I strongly recommend you **join the team track, no question**{:.yellow}! Instead of a long, lonely process on your own, grow through a fun journey together.
