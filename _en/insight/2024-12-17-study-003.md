## Study Jams?

> Get hands-on with Cloud Study Jams

Study Jams is a free, hands-on training program offered by Google Cloud. For developers who want to learn cloud technology, it provides __the chance to practice directly in a real GCP environment__{:.orange} through the Qwiklabs platform.

- Practice with about $300 worth of free credits provided by Google
- A wide range of hands-on courses for free (Kubernetes, BigQuery, Cloud Functions, AI, and more)
- Digital badges and certificates when you complete the labs
- Bonus Google Cloud goodies as extra rewards (T-shirts, stickers, etc.)

## Signing Up

You can check the Study Jams schedule on the [official site](https://sites.google.com/view/2024-study-jams/home?authuser=0){:target="_blank" .none}. It's also a good idea to join the [Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/studyjamkorea/){:target="_blank" .none} ahead of time. Once the recruitment period opens, you can join by filling out the ['Study Jams application form'](https://sites.google.com/view/2024-study-jams/ai-integration-class){:target="_blank" .none}. The recruitment period is fixed, so be sure to check it and apply early.

## First Thing to Do: Register Your Credits

When Study Jams starts, the very first thing to do is read the guide carefully and register your credits! Why? Because you need credits to do the labs. You have to get these credits before you can move on with the rest of the program.

![Register your credits first](/assets/img/content/insight/003/004.webp)

`> Registering your credits matters most!`{:.img-caption}

## Courses

You can study a variety of courses, step by step, from beginner to advanced. Study Jams designates specific courses, so to get the completion goodies you must finish those designated courses.

It runs for about a month, and you can study on your own whenever and as much as you like. Learning mainly happens in the following three ways.

- Video lectures alongside → better understanding
- Quizzes along the way to check what you've learned
- Labs based on real business scenarios

## Tips

### Facebook Group

- Be sure to join the ['Google Cloud Platform Korea'](https://www.facebook.com/groups/studyjamkorea){:target="_blank"} group on Facebook.
- Get the latest news, and get help if you run into trouble.

### Credits

- If you already have a GCP account, I recommend starting with a new one
- Be sure to activate your credits before starting the labs
- Leftover credits can be used to review courses or take other courses
- Credits expire after 6 months

### Other Tips

- You can retake quizzes even if you get them wrong
- For labs, you may need to wait a moment after finishing and then check again
- It's fine to play videos at 2x speed
- You can register your completion badges on [credly.com](credly.com){:target="_blank"}
- I recommend following the guide documents in English → for translation, I highly recommend the [Trancy Chrome extension](https://www.trancy.org/download){:target="_blank"}
