## Where Things Stand Today

Coding has become essential across nearly every industry, developers are in high demand, and anyone can now learn to code. As a result, lots of people are taking a shot at becoming developers. For someone just starting a developer career, the biggest wall is usually the coding test. At some point, **data structures and algorithms** started to feel like exam subjects you cram just to pass coding tests, but whether you want a developer job or simply want to keep growing, **there's no denying they matter**.

## Who This Book Is For

This book is not a coding test book. But unlike the many problem-driven coding test books out there, you can tell it puts much more effort into building an understanding of the fundamentals. It's for people who have just finished learning syntax, one step before studying for coding tests, and who want to **grow into better developers**. Think of it as the **rich, healthy soil** a tree needs in order to grow well.

## What I Liked

The book isn't intimidatingly thick, so it's easy to pick up. The **illustrations and explanations are remarkably detailed** when it comes to building intuition for concepts. Data structures and algorithms are hard largely because it's tough to study them while seeing what's going on, and you can feel how much effort went into solving exactly that.

## What I Missed

You **won't get to work through a wide variety of real coding test problems** using the data structures and algorithms it covers. As you'd expect, this one book can't prepare you for every coding test, and you'll definitely need to study more on top of it. Still, if you treat it as **preparation for the next book**, it does the job just fine.

## Final Thoughts

For those who have just learned basic Python syntax on the way to becoming developers, **the mountain you have to climb is the coding test**. If you've had even a small taste of coding tests before starting the climb, it probably feels daunting and overwhelming. This book serves as a **signpost for getting over that mountain**. You won't know how steep and grueling the coding test mountain really is until you're on it, but if you use this book as your signpost and prepare thoroughly before the climb, I believe you'll make it to the summit in the end.
