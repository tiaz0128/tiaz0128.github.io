## A Massive Shift

We've lived through many changes over the years.

- The internet era
- The smartphone era

But AI is bringing a **fundamental change** with an even bigger impact. People are amazed by this change and, at the same time, anxious about it. Why does the change AI brings feel different from the ones before? Probably because we feel that AI **threatens even our 'jobs.'**

## The Nature of the Change

So what is the fundamental change AI brings? Where does the anxiety that my job is at risk come from? It's this: **'it does what I do.'** AI is an amazing tool that handles my work for me. But I'm also afraid that this very advantage will turn into a double-edged sword and **end up threatening me.**

Using AI as a tool is now a massive, unavoidable trend of our time. It's an era where one person with AI can handle what used to take ten. The productivity gains from AI will bring enormous change to the labor market in every field. The era ahead is one where one person can do the work of ten: **an 'age of automation' in which individual productivity skyrockets through AI.**{:.yellow}

## If AI Writes Code for Us, Why Learn Python?

The days when programming belonged only to developers are over. People in all kinds of fields are using AI to solve all kinds of problems. So what does it take to use AI well?

At its core, AI works by answering questions. To get the results you want from AI, **you need to be able to ask good questions.**{:.orange} In other words, you need to understand Python's basic concepts and principles to give AI precise requirements and solve problems.

## Python's Strengths

People often say Python is the best language to start programming with. What makes it so good?

### Easy Syntax and Readability

Python's syntax resembles natural language, so even beginners can learn it quickly.

### Versatility

It can be used in almost every field: web development, data analysis, artificial intelligence, automation, scientific research, and more.

### Rich Libraries and Learning Resources

There are countless high-quality libraries, so you can easily find, install, and use whatever functionality you need. There's also a wealth of material to learn from (books, courses, blogs, references, open source...).

### Community

There's a huge developer community around the world, and here in Korea we have the **'Python Korea User Group.'** If you have questions or run into trouble with Python, ask for help!

## Where Python Shines

So in which fields is Python used?

### Backend Development & DevOps

You can build web service backends with frameworks like FastAPI, Django, and Flask. Python is also widely used for automation scripts, CI/CD pipeline management, cloud infrastructure management, and more.

### Data Science & Artificial Intelligence

Data analysis, machine learning, and deep learning with libraries like NumPy, Pandas, Scikit-learn, TensorFlow, and PyTorch are among Python's most powerful applications.

### **Work Automation**{:.orange}

To me, this is the hottest keyword. Every field is entering an era of automation through AI, and Python plays an important role as the bridge in between.

## The Age of Automation

Every field, and everyone, is thinking about how to automate their work. And I believe we're now in an era where, with AI and Python, **anyone can automate their work, developer or not**.

- "You can automatically generate posts and publish them to various platforms."
- "You can automate office work like checking email and creating documents."
- "You can hook into CCTV and automate anomaly detection."
- "You can even build automation systems that control factory equipment."

## Wrapping Up

Jensen Huang's remark above **emphasizes that people who use AI effectively will gain the competitive edge**, rather than AI technology replacing jobs outright. It suggests that adopting and adapting to AI quickly is the key to success going forward.

> Life is short, you need Python.

We live in a world where not only developers but non-developers too can do a great deal with the help of AI. **Python has gone beyond being just a programming language**; it's become a key that opens up countless possibilities.

With Python and AI, anyone can make their work more efficient. In the coming age of automation, building Python skills, whatever your field, is **an important key that expands your potential without limit.**{:.yellow}

"There's no better time to start learning Python than right now."
